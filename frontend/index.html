Methodology

Design principles

  • Actions over pledges — always. Net-zero commitments and safety statements are scored as pledges, not actions. Lobbying records, capital allocation, and verified outcomes determine stance.
  • No blended score. Domains are kept separate. A firm can be A-grade on climate and terrible on labor — that messiness is the story. Averaging hides it.
  • Tiered data confidence. Climate data is solid; AI-safety scoring is young; labor and tax are thinner. Each domain's column header shows its confidence tier.
  • Documented behavior only for individuals. Political donations, tax conduct, foundation payout rates — sourced facts only. Reader draws conclusions.

Pledge-vs-action gap

Every domain record carries a pledge_vs_action_gap field:

  • Severe — public commitment contradicted by documented action (e.g., net-zero pledge + active climate lobbying against policy).
  • Moderate — partial follow-through; pledges exceed verified action.
  • None — actions match or exceed stated commitments.
  • Unknown — insufficient data to assess.
